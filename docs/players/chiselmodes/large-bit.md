# Large Bit

![Large Bit icon](/icons/chiselmodes/cube_large.png)

Targets a large 8 × 8 × 8 cube of bits centered around the targeted position.

## Details

| Property  | Value       |
|-----------|-------------|
| Mode id   | `large_bit` |
| Group     | Cubed       |
| Selection | 8 × 8 × 8 bits |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits in the 8 × 8 × 8 cube and returns them to your inventory.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits in the 8 × 8 × 8 cube with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Ideal for rapidly clearing or filling large volumes — half a block's width in every direction.
