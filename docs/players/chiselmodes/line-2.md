# Line 2 x 2

![Line 2 icon](/icons/chiselmodes/line2.png)

Extends a 2 × 2 thick line from the targeted bit along the hit direction.

## Details

| Property  | Value     |
|-----------|-----------|
| Mode id   | `line_2`  |
| Group     | Line      |
| Thickness | 2 × 2 bits |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits in the 2 × 2 column extending in the opposite of the hit direction.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits in the 2 × 2 column extending in the hit direction.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Great for wide seams, conduits, or beams with a slight bit of thickness.
