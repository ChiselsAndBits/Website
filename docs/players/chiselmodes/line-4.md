# Line 4 x 4

![Line 4 icon](/icons/chiselmodes/line4.png)

Extends a 4 × 4 thick column from the targeted bit along the hit direction.

## Details

| Property  | Value     |
|-----------|-----------|
| Mode id   | `line_4`  |
| Group     | Line      |
| Thickness | 4 × 4 bits |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits in the 4 × 4 column in the opposite of the hit direction.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits in the 4 × 4 column in the hit direction.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Handy for quickly cutting quarter-block-wide shafts or pillars through a block.
