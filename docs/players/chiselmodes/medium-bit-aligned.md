# Medium Bit Aligned

![Medium Bit Aligned icon](/icons/chiselmodes/snap4.png)

A 4 × 4 × 4 cube that snaps to a fixed 1/4-block grid.

## Details

| Property  | Value                |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Mode id   | `medium_bit_aligned` |
| Group     | Cubed Aligned        |
| Selection | 4 × 4 × 4 bits, grid snapped to 1/4 |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes the aligned 4 × 4 × 4 cube at the snapped grid cell.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills the snapped quarter-block cell with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Quarter-block alignment — handy for tiles, brickwork or any pattern with quarter-block periodicity.
