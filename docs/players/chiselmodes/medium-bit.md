# Medium Bit

Targets a 4 × 4 × 4 cube of bits centered around the targeted position.

## Details

| Property  | Value        |
|-----------|--------------|
| Mode id   | `medium_bit` |
| Group     | Cubed        |
| Selection | 4 × 4 × 4 bits |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits in the 4 × 4 × 4 cube and returns them to your inventory.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits in the 4 × 4 × 4 area with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> A good balance between speed and precision for intermediate sculpting.
