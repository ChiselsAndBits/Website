# Plane 1 (Thin)

![Plane icon](/icons/chiselmodes/plane.png)

Edits the entire face of a block at the targeted depth, producing a flat 16 × 16 bit slab that is 1 bit deep.

## Details

| Property | Value     |
|----------|-----------|
| Mode id  | `plane_1` |
| Group    | Plane     |
| Depth    | 1 bit     |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits on the full face at depth 1 from the hit surface.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits on the face at depth 1 from the hit surface with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Use plane modes for adding or removing skins/layers from the surface of a chiseled block in one step.
