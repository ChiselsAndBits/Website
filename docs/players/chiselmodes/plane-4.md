# Plane 4

![Plane 4 icon](/icons/chiselmodes/plane4.png)

Edits a full 16 × 16 bit face slab that is 4 bits deep from the hit surface.

## Details

| Property | Value     |
|----------|-----------|
| Mode id  | `plane_4` |
| Group    | Plane     |
| Depth    | 4 bits    |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits on the face up to 4 bits deep (one quarter of the block).
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits within 4 bits of the face with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Cuts a quarter-block slab from the face in one click — handy for layered facades.
