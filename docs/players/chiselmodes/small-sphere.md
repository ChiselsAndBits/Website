# Small Sphere

![Small Sphere icon](/icons/chiselmodes/sphere_small.png)

Creates or removes a compact spherical volume. The sphere is centered on the targeted bit and has a diameter of 4 bits.

## Details

| Property  | Value          |
|-----------|----------------|
| Mode id   | `small_sphere` |
| Group     | Sphere         |
| Diameter  | 4 bits         |

## Usage

- **Left-click (chisel):** Removes all bits within the 4-bit-diameter sphere centered on the targeted bit.
- **Right-click (place):** Fills all air bits within the sphere with the held material.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Great for adding small round details like rivets, gems, or round indentations.
