### [Chisel Modes](chiselmodes/index.md)
All available chisel modes, grouped by category. Select a mode with `R` to open the radial menu.

- **Cubed** — single-click cube selections (1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 bits per side)
- **Cubed Aligned** — grid-snapped cube selections
- **Draw** — drag-to-define cuboids, lines and walls
- **Line** — full-column selections along the hit direction
- **Plane** — full-face slab selections at varying depth
- **Connected Plane** — face flood-fill limited to connected bits
- **Extrude** — connected-material flood-fill with depth
- **Sphere** — spherical volume selections
- **Replace** — swap all bits of one material for another
