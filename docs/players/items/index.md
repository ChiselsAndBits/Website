# Items

Tools and items you will use regularly while chiseling and building with Chisels & Bits.

## Available Items

### [Chisels](chisel.md)
The primary tool of the mod. Chisels let you remove or place individual bits on any supported block.
Available in several materials (Stone → Netherite), each with increased durability and speed.
Press `R` while holding a chisel to open the radial menu and select your active chisel mode.

### [Bit Bags](bit-bag.md)
Portable containers for carrying large amounts of bits without filling your entire inventory.
Bit Bags can be set to prefer a specific bit type and support various pickup-priority modes so they interact sensibly with your regular inventory.
